How a brand-new person is scored

  1. Book taste vector. The book factors are frozen. We solve a small ridge regression, u = argmin Σ(stars − vᵢ·u)² + 0.3‖u‖², so u is the 10-number taste that best explains the stars you gave.
  2. Two histories. 4–5★ books form the liked branch and 1–3★ books the disliked branch. Each branch builds a personal 10×10 bridge.
  3. Gate. α decides how much of each bridge to use for each movie. The semantic gate adds Jina text similarity between your books and that movie.
  4. Fallbacks. The probabilistic bridge and the uncertainty blend pull towards the movie's average when they're unsure about you. Their trust weight is w.
